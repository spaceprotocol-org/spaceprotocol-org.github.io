Mission statement

Space Protocol powers a trustless decentralized operating system for space. Satellites autonomously sense, share, and react, delivering real-time risk quantification and orbital compliance. This ensures secure, traceable data, liability coverage, and a trusted marketplace for cross-verified space intelligence—driving efficiency and trust for space operations.
